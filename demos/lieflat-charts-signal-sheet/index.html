Field notes · 8.7 / 10

Strong taste, strict contracts, real friction.

Three things it does well

  1. It makes data shape and reading speed decide the chart before visual taste enters.
  2. Its countable rungs, dots, and hairlines keep small datasets detailed without inventing records.
  3. Template receipts, source lines, replayable motion, and reduced-motion handling make the output inspectable.

Three things to improve

  1. The PolyForm Noncommercial license needs an explicit commercial-use decision before client work.
  2. Catalog IDs and implementation comments sometimes drift, which slows precise template retrieval.
  3. The mandatory candidate audit is valuable but heavy for quick, low-stakes charts.

Useful day to day

  • Turn a small survey into a countable editorial visual.
  • Make a restrained portfolio, annual-review, or research chart page.
  • Give an agent a disciplined chart-selection process instead of a vague “make it pretty” prompt.
