Positioning thesis

For practical people exploring AI agent workflows, Agent Skills Phoebe Picks is the evidence-first Skill gallery that shows what a Skill changes through a real build - unlike lists and leaderboards that stop at description or popularity.

Who it is for

Curious practitioners who need fewer, stronger options.

  • Practical builders
  • Data and AI practitioners
  • Team and executive leads
  • Students and new adopters
The tension

Discovery is abundant. Evidence is scarce.

People can find thousands of Skills but struggle to judge authenticity, outcome impact, drift, portability, and safety.

Why this shelf

The artifact is the review.

  • Exact source and path
  • Ground-up proof
  • Concept coverage
  • Three strengths and three limits
Who it is not for

No universal ranking. No automatic trust.

Not for anyone seeking guaranteed safety, professional advice, automatic installation, or claims that one test proves every environment.
