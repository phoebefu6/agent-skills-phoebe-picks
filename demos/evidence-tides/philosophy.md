# Evidence Tides

Evidence Tides treats every promising idea as a body moving through an invisible field. Questions enter at the edges, drift through uncertainty, and gather around small islands of proof. The artwork is never composed as a fixed poster. It is a living system whose visual hierarchy emerges from thousands of local decisions made by particles following mathematical forces.

The field combines layered noise with a quiet rotational current. Noise creates regional weather: slow basins, energetic channels, and seams where direction changes. A small set of seeded evidence wells bends those currents without fully controlling them. The result should feel like disciplined inquiry rather than decoration - a meticulously crafted algorithm where uncertainty is allowed to move, but never without structure.

Each particle carries a short memory. Its trail records where the system has been, while gradual fading prevents history from overwhelming the present. Velocity controls brightness and temperature, so decisive movement becomes warm and visible while hesitation remains cool and faint. The composition should reveal its logic slowly, the product of deep computational expertise and painstaking tuning rather than undirected random noise.

Seeded variation is the soul of the movement. Every seed redistributes the evidence wells, shifts the field phase, and changes the routes by which particles discover coherence. Yet the rules remain stable enough that the same seed always produces the same world. Parameter changes alter density, turbulence, pull, and memory as meaningful properties of the system, not as a menu of unrelated visual effects.

The work is complete only when complexity feels inevitable. Empty space must have purpose, crowded regions must breathe, and no current should exist merely to add spectacle. Evidence Tides aims for master-level implementation: controlled chaos refined through repeated observation, careful performance choices, and countless small calibrations until every emergent frame feels intentional.
