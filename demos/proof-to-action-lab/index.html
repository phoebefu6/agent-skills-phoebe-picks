Questions before you trust the shelf

Good skepticism belongs here.

Does one field test prove a Skill works everywhere?

No. It shows what happened in one bounded scenario. The artifact, coverage, and gaps help you judge whether the evidence transfers.

Are GitHub stars treated as a quality score?

No. Repository popularity is only a publication credibility gate. It is kept separate from the hands-on field rating.

Does “published” mean automatically safe to install?

No. Review the source, permissions, dependencies, and fit for your environment. The gallery does not provide a universal safety guarantee.
