Phoebe's verdict

Strong visual thinking.
One raster caveat.

The workflow earns its keep by separating information architecture from art direction and saving the complete prompt. Small image-model typography still needs a human eye.

What works

  1. Structure and style are separate decisions.
  2. The saved prompt makes revision practical.
  3. Inspection is part of the workflow, not an afterthought.

What to improve

  1. Small generated labels can soften or drift.
  2. The setup is heavy for low-stakes visual notes.
  3. Responsive crops need clearer guidance.
