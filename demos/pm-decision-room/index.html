Final verdict

A complete PM operating system—if evidence remains more important than framework volume.

Exactly 3 strengths
  1. Connects market learning, product choices, execution, measurement, and launch without losing the reasoning chain.
  2. Turns vague PM judgment into inspectable artifacts, thresholds, trade-offs, and follow-up decisions.
  3. Works across Claude Code, Codex, and other Skill-aware agents because the core Skills use portable markdown instructions.
Exactly 3 improvements
  1. Needs a first-class provenance field that distinguishes sourced facts, modeled data, estimates, assumptions, and AI inference.
  2. Needs shorter recommended paths so users do not apply overlapping canvases merely because they exist.
  3. Needs more explicit stop rules when research quality is too weak to complete a framework responsibly.
